Dover Sole
1 x 6/800g Dover sole
Clean and dry the fish well. Remove the head, clean the cavity, then cut down the centre from head to tail without cutting through. Use scissors to cut through the bones and remove the spine. Rinse in cold water, drain and store on dry kitchen towel.

Fish Fumet
500g clean, flat fish frames, washed and free of blood
2 celery stalks
½ onion
½ leek
Soft fine herbs – parsley/dill/tarragon
½ lemon
1 star anise
-
Wash frames until clean. Place in a heavy-based stock pan with cut vegetables, bring to the boil, skim well and simmer 20 minutes, skimming often. Remove from heat, add herbs, sliced lemon and star anise, then infuse 20 minutes. Pass carefully through muslin and chinois, leaving sediment behind.

Mushroom Stock
500g chestnut mushroom trim
500g water
½ bulb garlic split
3g salt
-
Blitz mushroom trim in robot coupe until coarse. Add to stockpot and add water/salt/garlic. Bring to a boil and reduce heat to barely a simmer for 20 minutes, pass over colander with muslin. Reduce by half/until intense.

Mushroom Puree
750g chestnut mushroom, sliced
2 shallots, sliced
6 cloves garlic, microplaned
125g butter
200ml madeira
20ml sherry vinegar
300ml mushroom stock
150ml double cream
150ml milk
-
Brown butter, saute mushrooms with good colour. Add shallots, garlic and a pinch of salt. Soften. De glaze with vinegar, then madeira, then mushroom stock, then cover with cream and milk and cook for 20 minutes. Rest. Blend until completely smooth and emulsified. Pass and check.

Braising Fish
Filleted dover sole on the bone
1 banana shallot, peeled and sliced
10g thyme
20ml soy sauce
20ml truffle juice
300ml mushroom stock
300ml fish stock
20g butter
Salt
-
Preheat your oven to 170c.
Place fish in a pan large enough to braise the fish comfortably.
Add the remaining ingredients and cover with a cartouche.
Transfer to the oven and braise for 8/12 minutes depending on the size of the fish. Allow to rest in the liquor for 6/8 minutes.
Remove the fish from the pan and reserve on a plate.
Begin to reduce the cooking liquor, aiming to reduce by 2/3rds.
Finish the sauce with double cream and crème fraiche and reduce by half.
Remove the fish from the bone, making sure to remove any small bones.
Reheat gently if needed, but the heat of the hot sauce should suffice.
-
Pass sauce through a fine sieve and finish with finely cut chives.
Nape over the fish and serve with mushroom puree, garnished with pickled mushrooms and sea purslane.
