I wanted to see if I could cook two duck breasts all the way on the skin side and portion with the crispy skin still intact. 
 
Duck Butchery
Bone out the duck for the galantine reserving the skin with breasts attached. Remove leg meat for another preparation. Trim into a large square with excess skin. 
You can find this butchery method in further detail in my reels below.
 
Serves four people
 
Ingredients;
1 Aylesbury duck
2 oranges segmented , reserve the skin from one orange with a peeler
25g caster sugar
50ml Cointreau
3g pink peppercorns
500ml duck stock
2 tsp chopped chives
250g sweet potato puree
 
Method
Cook the duck breasts skin side down starting in a cold pan that is seasoned with salt. Cook slowly until the skin is crisp, basting the breast meat with the fat that renders out.
 
It’s the sauce that’s important here.
Whilst the duck rests, Use the pan from the duck cooking if you can. Add a few orange peelings and some pink peppercorns and a spoon full of sugar.
Caramelise this before deglazing with the Cointreau and then go in with the orange juice (this is where the sweet and sour comes from) 
Once reduced add some good duck stock and reduce to a nice glaze. 
Add a knob of butter and simmer the sauce.
Now pass the sauce through a fine sieve and finish with some fresh segments.
 
For this dish I have used a sweet potato purée and kept things very simple.
 
Give it a go, use a duck breast if the butchery feels daunting.
